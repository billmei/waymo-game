How to Play

You are the robotaxi pit crew. When a car near you pulls over with a bubble over its roof, run to it and fix the problem right there on the street before the rider runs out of patience. You have three shifts of 100 seconds.

  • WashHold and drag the sponge to scrub off mud and bird droppings.
  • RechargeDrag the plug to the port, then tap when the needle is in the green.
  • De-coneSomeone put a cone on the hood again. Grab it and fling it away.
  • Shoo birdsPigeons love warm sensors. Tap them to send them off. Gulls take two.
  • RebootFog confuses the lidar. Key in the recalibration code with the arrows.
  • VacuumThe roof comes off! Sweep the vacuum over the crumbs, popcorn and lost socks.
  • Close doorsRiders left the doors open. Hop on your skateboard and slam them shut.
